How it runs in your course

From your assignment to graded evidence, in four steps.

1

Adapt

Bring one legacy assignment. We reshape it together: same learning goals, your rubric, plus a planted difficulty a blind copy-paste gets wrong.

2

Session

Students work in an AI-allowed session inside the workspace, recorded with consent. No video recording, no lockdown browser.

3

Two scores

Every score cites a moment in the record. The work on your rubric, the AI use on the published Delegator, Operator, Director scale.

4

Confirm & export

You confirm every outcome, then export the grades for your LMS gradebook. Canvas LTI 1.3 with grade passback is coming.

What students leave with

A grade for the course. Proof for the career.

Feedback pointers

After every assignment, students see in plain language where they delegated blindly and where a check would have caught an error. Responsible AI use is taught, not just demanded.

A verified AI-competency result

Each assignment produces a verifiable credential (Open Badges 3.0) and a shareable result page, dated and explained. Real evidence behind the "AI skills" line on a résumé.

Levels that grow with them

Measured again in every course on the same scale, from Delegator to Operator to Director. Growth becomes visible across a degree, and the university's output becomes readable from the outside.

Built for the classroom

No proctoring. No prohibition.

AI allowed by design

Nothing to police, nothing to ban. The integrity question moves from "did they use it" to "how well did they use it."

Consent-first recording

Workspace-only. No video recording, no screen capture, no keystroke logging outside the task.

Humans confirm outcomes

Low-confidence results route to you, every outcome is confirmed in person, and students can dispute.

Your rubric stays yours

The collaboration scale is added alongside your criteria, never instead.
