What is Stima

sti·ma
/ˈstiː.ma/ · Italian · noun
two meanings, one word
  1. assessment, valuation. the act of weighing what something is worth
  2. esteem, regard. the act of holding someone in high worth

Stima is a career navigation and upskilling platform with post-AI skills assessment at its core.

It maps where you are, charts where you are going, and verifies what you know along the way, including how well you work with AI, so every step of your career is measured, meaningful, and yours to prove.

The name is the mission: measuring what people can do should raise how they are valued, not reduce them to a score.

About Stima

A career is a journey, and every journey deserves a reliable instrument.

Stima started with two obsessions: how people find their way, and how they prove what they find along it.

In an age when AI can produce any answer on demand, the skill that matters is no longer producing the answer. It is directing the machine: knowing what to ask, what to trust, and when to take over. We are building the instrument that measures exactly that.
